## Temperature → layering

| °C | Outer | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ≤ 5 | long wool / puffer coat | knit + scarf, closed shoes, tights |
| 6 – 12 | long coat, trench + knit | loafers / boots |
| 13 – 19 | trench, blazer, suede / leather jacket | light knit, sneakers or loafers |
| ≥ 20 | none | linen, dresses, sandals, flats |

## Formula grammar

`<outer> + <top> + <bottom> + <shoes> [+ <accessory>]`, each piece `<color> [material] <garment>`.
Summer formulas may drop `<outer>`; a dress fills top + bottom.
Search text = the pieces joined by commas, without the role words.
